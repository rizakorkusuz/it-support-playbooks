# Audio-Visual and Conference Room Support

Meeting rooms are where support is most visible: a failed display costs a room full of people their time. Preparation, a fast checklist and clear handoffs matter more than deep AV engineering.

## 1. Know the room

For each room keep a one-page sheet: display or projector model, inputs (HDMI, USB-C, wireless casting), the room panel or remote, the microphone and speaker setup, the camera, the network connection, the vendor contact and the room owner. Photograph the cabling. Note how to restart the room system safely.

## 2. Pre-meeting check (5 minutes)

- [ ] Display on, correct input, no warning messages
- [ ] Laptop output shows on the display (HDMI, USB-C or casting)
- [ ] Microphone picks up voice, speaker volume is comfortable, no echo
- [ ] Camera shows the right view and is not covered
- [ ] Teams or Zoom room or laptop signed in, test call works
- [ ] Spare cables and adapters in the room, remote batteries working
- [ ] Network connected (wired preferred for rooms)

## 3. Troubleshooting guide

| Symptom | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| Display says "No signal" | Input selection, reseat cable, swap cable or adapter, Windows+P (Duplicate or Extend), try another laptop |
| Picture is cropped or fuzzy | Resolution and scaling on the laptop, display aspect setting, cable quality, adapter limits |
| Display works but no audio in room | Audio output device on the laptop, HDMI audio selected, room volume and mute, speaker power |
| Echo or feedback | Only one microphone and speaker active, laptop speaker muted, someone joined from two devices in the same room |
| Microphone not heard | Mute states, selected microphone in the meeting app, OS privacy permissions, battery in wireless microphones |
| Camera not available | Selected camera, privacy permissions, another app using it, USB port, firmware |
| Wireless casting fails | Both on the same network, casting service enabled, restart casting, use cable as fallback |
| Meeting cannot start | Sign-in or license problem, network, meeting link or calendar, join from the web |
| Room panel unresponsive | Follow the room-owner procedure, then contact the AV vendor |

## 4. Fallback plan

Always keep a quick fallback: a known-good HDMI cable and USB-C adapter, a portable speakerphone, or move the meeting to another room. Tell the organizer what you are doing and how long it will take.

## 5. During a live meeting

Stay calm and quiet. Fix the most visible problem first. Apologize briefly, avoid technical talk, and report what you changed. Do not restart systems in the middle of a meeting unless the organizer agrees.

## 6. After the meeting

Tidy cables, record any failed component, and open a ticket for the vendor or facilities if needed. Add a KB article if a problem repeats.

## 7. Vendor coordination

When contacting a vendor, give the room, equipment model and serial number, symptoms, steps tried, impact and next meeting time. Ask for a ticket number and an estimated response time, and update the requester. Keep the warranty and service contract details with the room sheet.

## 8. Safety and etiquette

Use a ladder only if authorized and trained. Do not lift heavy equipment alone. Keep cables out of walkways, and do not leave personal data on shared room computers: sign out of accounts after use.

## 9. Scope

Basic room support covers cables, inputs, audio and video settings and meeting apps. Control system programming, ceiling microphone tuning and projector installation belong to AV integrators or the vendor.
